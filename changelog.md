# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Added RealmFinder Settings. Press **O** to open it; hover over a setting for a short explanation.
- Choose whether copied chests keep their items and mobs keep their gear.
- Customize blank photo costs, camera wear, obstacle clearing, capture limits, and Undo history.
- Added personal options for Undo confirmation, camera zoom, and HUD hints.
- In multiplayer, server operators control gameplay rules for everyone.
- Supported Fabric/Forge 1.20.1 and Fabric/Neoforge 1.21.1
