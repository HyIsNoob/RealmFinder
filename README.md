# RealmFinder

Bring your photographs to life. Inspired by the indie puzzle game *Viewfinder*, RealmFinder lets you capture slices of the Minecraft world with a camera and stamp them back into reality from your own perspective.

> Note: Early Alpha Release. This is an early preview build released to test core mechanics and collect player feedback. Bugs and rapid changes are to be expected.

---

## How to Play

1. Grab a Viewfinder Camera from the Tools and Utilities tab (or craft one).
2. Look at any building, terrain, or group of animals and Right-click to take a snapshot.
3. Hold the Photograph in your hand to see the real-time perspective overlay appear on your screen.
4. Line up your view and Right-click to stamp the photograph into reality!
5. Made a mistake? Press Z anytime to undo and recover your photo.

---

## Features

### Reshape Your World
Snap a photo of a bridge, rotate around, and stamp it across a canyon. What you see through the photograph becomes real blocks in the world.

### Capture Living Mobs
Animals, villagers, monsters, and armor stands in your photo frame are captured and recreated when you stamp the photo into the world.

### Resize Reality
Hold Ctrl or Shift and scroll your mouse wheel (or use the [ and ] bracket keys) while holding a photograph to change its scale:
- 0.5x Miniature
- 1.0x Normal
- 2.0x Giant
- 3.0x Colossal

### Clean Undo Anytime
Mistakes happen. Press the Z key to immediately reverse your last photo placement. Blocks return to normal, spawned entities disappear, and your Photograph item returns straight to your inventory.

### Photo Album
Right-click the Photo Album item to open a two-page gallery. Store up to 18 photographs on the left page and inspect full-size live photo previews with capture dates, block counts, and mob counts on the right page.

### Photo Display Stand
Place a wooden easel stand in your world to display your best shots in 3D. Right-click with a photo to mount it, right-click with an empty hand to take it back, or Shift + Right-click to materialize the photo forward into the world directly from the easel.

## Controls

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Take photograph | Right-click with Camera |
| Stamp photo into world | Right-click with Photograph |
| Additive stamp (no carving) | Shift + Right-click with Photograph |
| Snap angle lock (90 degrees) | Hold Shift while holding Photograph |
| Change photo size | Ctrl / Shift + Mouse Scroll (or [ and ] keys) |
| Undo last placement | Press Z |
| Open Photo Album | Right-click with Photo Album |
| Mount / take photo on easel | Right-click Photo Stand |
| Stamp photo from easel | Shift + Right-click Photo Stand |
